============================================================
1. PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

CivicAI is a citizen-centric civic grievance reporting and resolution
platform designed for Tamil Nadu urban/local government services.

Citizens can:

- Report civic problems
- Report anonymously
- Report in Tamil or English
- Upload photos/videos
- Capture GPS location
- Select location manually
- Use AI assistance
- Track complaints
- View complaint status and timeline
- Receive notifications
- View public civic issue heatmaps
- View public transparency statistics
- Give feedback
- Reopen unresolved complaints
- Chat with CivicAI

Government departments can:

- Receive complaints
- Review complaints
- Review AI recommendations
- Assign complaints
- Monitor SLA
- Escalate delayed complaints
- Manage field officers
- Verify resolution
- View analytics
- Monitor live civic issues

Field officers can:

- View assigned complaints
- Navigate to complaint location
- Accept assignments
- Capture before photos
- Update progress
- Capture after photos
- Submit resolution
- Work with poor network connectivity

Administrators can:

- Monitor all departments
- Monitor complaint volume
- Monitor SLA
- View heatmaps
- View live operational status
- Review AI verification queue
- Manage departments
- Manage officers
- View audit information
- View analytics
